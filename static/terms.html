Terms

Use this as a DIY helper, not a pro inspection

Room Rescue looks at a photo and drafts a checklist. It is a small tool for homeowners who want a practical starting list. It is not a licensed inspection, contractor estimate, diagnosis, or professional advice.

The list can be wrong

The checklist is generated by an AI vision model. It only sees what is in the picture you send — and even then it can miss things, invent details, or misread a surface, stain, or fixture. If something does not match the room in front of you, trust the room.

Safety and when to call a pro

You are responsible for how you use the list. Stop and get qualified help for electrical work, gas, structural issues, substantial water intrusion, suspected mold, asbestos, lead, pests you cannot identify, or anything that feels unsafe. Do not treat a photo note as proof that a hazard is or isn’t there.

If there is smoke, a gas smell, standing water near power, or someone is hurt, leave and use emergency services. This site is not a substitute for that.

Who it is for

This tool is meant for adults doing ordinary home upkeep. It is not directed at children.

No promises about the list

Room Rescue is provided as-is. We do not promise the checklist is complete, accurate, or safe to follow. You decide what to do in your home.

Photos and this browser

Photos are sent to analyze a room, then discarded on our side. Checkmarks stay in this browser. See Privacy for the short version.
