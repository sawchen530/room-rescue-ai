### Description (4000)

```
Photograph a room. Room Rescue drafts a practical checklist for what to clean, fix, organize, or inspect — with a time estimate and ordinary supplies — then you can take an after photo from the same spot to check what’s visible.

Built for DIY homeowners who want a starting list. No account, no payments, no ads, and no photo library.

How it works
1. Photograph the room from an angle you can come back to.
2. Work the checklist. Check off items on this device.
3. Take an after photo to see what the new picture can show.

Don’t want to upload a photo yet? Use a sample room. That uses our bundled living-room picture, not yours.

The list is generated by an AI vision model from what is visible in your photo. It can miss things, invent details, or be wrong. Trust the room in front of you. This is not a licensed inspection, diagnosis, estimate, or professional advice. Stop and call a qualified person for electrical work, gas, structural issues, water, or anything that feels unsafe.

Photos are sent to our server in memory, then to OpenAI to analyze the room. We do not keep your photos as a product feature. Checkmarks stay on the device.

Privacy: https://room-rescue-ai-production.up.railway.app/privacy
Terms: https://room-rescue-ai-production.up.railway.app/terms
```
