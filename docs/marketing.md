### Description

```
Room Rescue is a small web tool for DIY homeowners. Photograph a room from an angle you can come back to. It drafts a starting checklist for what to clean, fix, organize, or inspect — with a time estimate and ordinary supplies — then you can take an after photo from the same spot to check what’s visible.

It is a helper, not a licensed inspection, diagnosis, or professional advice. The list is generated from what’s in the photo and can miss things, invent details, or be wrong. Trust the room in front of you.

No account, no payments, no ads, and no photo library. Don’t want to upload a picture of your house? Use a sample living room.

Photos are sent to our server in memory, then to OpenAI to analyze the room. We do not keep photos as a product feature. Checkmarks stay in this browser.

This is the live website. It is not on the App Store or Play Store yet.

https://room-rescue-ai-production.up.railway.app
```
